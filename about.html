Who I Am

I have a strong interest in language, technology, and creativity, and I enjoy discovering how these areas can work together to create innovative ideas and solutions.

Throughout my academic journey, I have developed an appreciation for continuous learning, personal development, and challenging myself to improve. I believe that every learning experience provides an opportunity to build new skills, broaden perspectives, and become a more capable individual.

02

Why I Chose This Field

I chose Linguistic and Information Technology because I am fascinated by the relationship between human language and modern technology. I find it interesting how technology can be used to understand language, improve communication, process information, and address various real-world challenges.

What makes this field particularly valuable is its combination of two different yet complementary disciplines. It provides opportunities to explore areas such as computational linguistics, natural language processing, language analysis, information systems, and digital innovation. This combination allows me to develop both analytical and technical perspectives, while understanding the importance of language in an increasingly digital world.
